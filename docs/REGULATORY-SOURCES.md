## 1. Primary Legislation

### Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act)
- **Type**: EU Regulation
- **Adopted**: 13 June 2024
- **Published**: Official Journal L 2024/1689, 12.7.2024
- **Entry into force**: 1 August 2024
- **Key provisions**:
  - Risk-based approach (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal)
  - Transparency obligations
  - Human oversight requirements
  - Technical documentation
  - Post-market monitoring
- **Source**: [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689)
- **Status**: In force

### Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- **Type**: EU Regulation
- **Adopted**: 27 April 2016
- **Applicable from**: 25 May 2018
- **Key principles**:
  - Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
  - Purpose limitation
  - Data minimisation
  - Accuracy
  - Storage limitation
  - Integrity and confidentiality
  - Accountability
- **Source**: [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)
- **Status**: In force

## 3. CE Marking — AI Act Article 48

### Investigation Result
- **Article**: AI Act Article 48 (Presumption of conformity)
- **Scope**: CE marking applies to high-risk AI systems that have undergone conformity assessment
- **Applicability to OPUS67**: **REQUIRES ASSESSMENT**
  - OPUS67 is currently an MVP
  - No AI system has been deployed that would require CE marking
  - CE marking cannot be applied simply because a product involves AI
  - Conformity assessment by a notified body may be required for high-risk systems
- **Decision**: Do NOT display CE marking on OPUS67 until proper assessment is completed

## 4. GDPR Certification — Article 42

### Investigation Result
- **Article**: GDPR Article 42 (Certification)
- **Mechanism**: Voluntary certification through approved certification bodies
- **Current status of OPUS67**: No GDPR certification obtained
- **Decision**: Do NOT claim "GDPR Certified" or display any GDPR certification mark
