## EU Emblem

### Asset Information
- **File**: `/public/regulatory/eu/eu-emblem.svg`
- **Type**: SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics)
- **Dimensions**: 810 × 540 (3:2 ratio)
- **Colours**:
  - Background: #003399 (Pantone Reflex Blue)
  - Stars: #FFCC00 (Pantone Yellow)
- **Description**: Official reproduction of the European Union emblem featuring 12 five-pointed gold stars arranged in a circle on a blue background.

### Official Source
- **URL**: https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/symbols/european-flag_en
- **Direct download (SVG)**: https://european-union.europa.eu/document/download/8fd15c1e-c5b8-44e7-a9eb-824609d68150_en?filename=Normal%20reproduction%20in%20colour%20-%20SVG.zip
- **Graphical guide**: https://style-guide.europa.eu/en/content/-/isg/topic?identifier=annex-a1-graphics-guide-european-emblem

### Usage Rights
- **Permission**: Third parties may use the EU emblem without written permission, provided:
  1. Use does not create impression of connection with EU institutions where none exists
  2. Use does not lead public to believe user benefits from EU support/sponsorship/approval
  3. Use is not incompatible with EU aims and principles
- **Restrictions**:
  - Cannot be registered as trademark
  - Cannot imply endorsement or approval
  - Must maintain official proportions and colours
  - Cannot be modified

### OPUS67 Usage
- **Purpose**: Institutional/regulatory reference to indicate alignment with European regulatory framework
- **Context**: Used in the European AI Governance section of the home page
- **Disclaimer**: Accompanied by clear text stating that use does not imply EU endorsement
- **Legal basis**: Administrative agreement published in Official Journal (2012/C 271/04)

### Status
✅ **OFFICIAL SOURCE VERIFIED**
